This article examines how online ordering platforms must evolve to serve multi-location restaurant brands effectively, explaining that as restaurant groups grow from one to ten-plus locations, workflows that worked for single stores break down, requiring brand-level menu management, consolidated reporting, and unified customer data. ChowNow's commission-free direct ordering model is positioned as a solution that keeps customer relationships and data with the brand rather than fragmenting across locations or third-party platforms.
